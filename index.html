What to watch

Only show videos with captions

Fewer videos, but more of them can show subtitles. Use the CC button on a video to switch between Fidel and English. Each only shows when the video has captions in that language.

Play longer videos as clips

In Shorts, Mix and Following, videos over 3 minutes play a 1–3 minute part after the intro. Tap Keep watching for the full video. Long always shows full videos.

Clear watched history

Hidden channels

Channels you marked "No talking" on a video. Their videos stay out of your feed.

Your data

Back up your words and practice, clips, saved videos, ratings, and listening time, move them to another device, or share them with your tutor or Claude.

Importing adds to what's here. Nothing on this device gets deleted.

How to get your own free API key
  1. Open console.cloud.google.com and create a project.
  2. Go to APIs & Services → Library, search YouTube Data API v3, and enable it.
  3. Go to APIs & Services → Credentials → Create credentials → API key.
  4. Restrict it: set Websites to this app's address, and limit it to YouTube Data API v3.
  5. Paste it above.

A key gets 10,000 free units a day. A feed refill uses about 100–200, and creators you follow cost almost nothing.
